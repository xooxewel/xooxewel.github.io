Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the online ruler?

After calibrating against a bank card, readings are typically within about 1mm over 10cm. Uncalibrated, screens can be off by 10% or more because browsers can't report true physical pixel density.

Why do I need to calibrate at all?

Browsers only expose CSS pixels, which assume a fixed 96-pixel-per-inch convention, not your screen's real density. Calibrating against a standard ID-1 card (85.6mm wide) corrects for your specific device.

Does calibration work on phones and tablets, not just desktop monitors?

Yes — calibration works by matching a real card on screen, so it behaves the same on any device. It only needs to be redone if you switch to a different physical screen.

How to calibrate an online ruler

Follow these steps to improve measurement accuracy:

  1. Open the online ruler on your device.
  2. Make sure your browser zoom is set to 100%.
  3. Locate the ruler calibration option, if available.
  4. Compare the displayed ruler with a physical object of a known size.
  5. Adjust the screen scale until the displayed measurement matches the reference.
  6. Check the ruler again before measuring other objects.

A standard bank card can also be used as a reference because standard ID-1 cards measure 85.60 mm × 53.98 mm.

Important: Do not assume an online ruler is accurate simply because it displays inch or centimeter markings. Always verify the physical scale before using it for measurements that require precision.

Why does an online ruler sometimes show the wrong size?

An online ruler may appear larger or smaller than its intended physical dimensions because different devices use different display configurations. Common reasons include:

  • Incorrect screen calibration.
  • Browser zoom settings.
  • Operating system display scaling.
  • Different monitor sizes and resolutions.
  • Changes in display settings.

Recalibrating the ruler and checking your browser settings can help resolve these issues.

How to convert inches to centimeters

To convert inches into centimeters, multiply the inch measurement by 2.54.
Formula: Centimeters = Inches × 2.54
Example: 5 inches × 2.54 = 12.7 cm.

How to convert centimeters to inches

To convert centimeters into inches, divide the centimeter measurement by 2.54.
Formula: Inches = Centimeters ÷ 2.54
Example: 20 cm ÷ 2.54 = approximately 7.87 inches.

How to convert millimeters to centimeters

To convert millimeters into centimeters, divide the measurement by 10.
Formula: Centimeters = Millimeters ÷ 10
Example: 50 mm ÷ 10 = 5 cm.

How to convert centimeters to millimeters

To convert centimeters into millimeters, multiply the measurement by 10.
Formula: Millimeters = Centimeters × 10
Example: 8 cm × 10 = 80 mm.
